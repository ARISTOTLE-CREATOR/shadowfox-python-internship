# HR Attrition Executive Dashboard — Power BI Build Specification

## Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Overview
KPI cards:
- Total Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Attrition Count
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Age
- Overtime Employees

Visuals:
- Attrition Rate by Department
- Employee Count by Department
- Attrition Rate by Overtime
- Attrition Rate by Business Travel
- Department → Job Role matrix with Employees, Attrition Count and Attrition Rate

Slicers:
Department, Job Role, Gender, Education, Overtime, Business Travel.

### Page 2 — Attrition Drivers
- Attrition Rate by Job Satisfaction
- Attrition Rate by Environment Satisfaction
- Attrition Rate by Years at Company
- Attrition Rate by Age band
- Attrition Rate by Monthly Income band
- Attrition by Business Travel and Overtime
- Department → Job Role drill-down

### Page 3 — Management Insights
Show the major patterns, workforce profile, decision-oriented recommendations and a methodology/data-quality note.

## Design
Executive corporate layout, clean light canvas, dark text, one accent color, consistent KPI cards, clear hierarchy, no 3D charts.

## Dataset disclosure
Synthetic dataset created for internship demonstration. Patterns are descriptive and are not causal claims.
